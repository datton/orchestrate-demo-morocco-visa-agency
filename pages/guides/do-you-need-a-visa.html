Do you need a visa for Morocco?

It depends on your passport. Here is what is confirmed, and what to check with the embassy. Rules checked 20.11.2026.

What is confirmed

For UK passport holders (full British citizen passports), the UK government says you can visit Morocco without a visa for up to 90 days for tourism. For other purposes, check with the Moroccan consulate.

  • Your passport must expire at least 3 months after your arrival date (UK travellers).
  • Stays beyond 90 days need an extension request at a Moroccan police station.
  • Make sure your passport is stamped on entry. Travellers without a stamp have had trouble leaving.
  • Foreign currency of 100,000 MAD or more must be declared on entry or exit.
  • A polio vaccination certificate is required if you arrive from a polio-affected country.
  • Never post passports to or through Morocco: it is illegal and they are confiscated.

Source: UK FCDO, Morocco entry requirements. Rules checked 20.11.2026.

Check with the embassy

We could not confirm these from an official source on 20.11.2026, so we do not state them as facts:

  • Which nationalities need a visa. Check whether your passport needs a visa with the Moroccan embassy or consulate.
  • Online e-visa. Morocco runs an e-visa for eligible nationalities. Check eligibility, fees and processing times on the official portal or with the embassy.
  • Passport validity. Sources differ. Most travellers need at least 3 to 6 months of validity: check your embassy.
  • Fees, processing times and document lists. Ask the embassy or consulate.

Steps

  1. Find your passport's expiry date and your planned arrival date.
  2. Ask the Moroccan embassy or consulate whether your passport needs a visa for your purpose of travel.
  3. If a visa or e-visa is needed, follow the official portal or consulate instructions.
  4. Before you travel, check passport validity again and the current official guidance.

What to prepare

  • Your passport and its expiry date.
  • Your travel dates and purpose of travel.
  • Any documents the embassy asks for (we do not state a list here).
