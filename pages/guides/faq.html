Morocco visa FAQ

Short answers. Where we could not confirm an official source we say so and point you to the embassy.

Do I need a visa for Morocco?

UK passport holders (full British citizen passports) can visit without a visa for up to 90 days for tourism. For any other passport, check whether it needs a visa with the Moroccan embassy or consulate. Other purposes: check with the consulate.

Is there an e-visa, and what does it cost?

Morocco is reported to run an online e-visa for eligible nationalities, but we could not open the official portal on 20.11.2026, so we do not state its conditions, fees or timings. Check eligibility and the official fee on the official portal or with the embassy.

How long must my passport be valid?

For UK travellers, it must expire at least 3 months after arrival (UK FCDO). Other sources give 3 to 6 months, so most travellers should check with their embassy.

What if I want to stay more than 90 days?

You need an extension request at a Moroccan police station. See our guide to staying longer.

Why does the entry stamp matter?

Travellers without a stamp have had trouble leaving. Make sure your passport is stamped when you arrive.

Can I post my passport to Morocco?

No. Passports must not be posted to or through Morocco: it is illegal and they are confiscated.

How much cash can I bring?

Foreign currency of 100,000 MAD or more must be declared on entry or exit.

Do I need a vaccination certificate?

A polio vaccination certificate is required if you arrive from a polio-affected country. For other health requirements, check with the embassy.

Are you the Moroccan government or an embassy?

No. We help with forms, checklists and guidance. The visa decision is Morocco's, and we cannot promise approval or speed. Our prices cover our help only, never official fees.
