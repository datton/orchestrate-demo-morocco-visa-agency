Step by step

  1. Check whether your passport needs a visa

    Rules depend on nationality. For example, UK holders of a full British citizen passport can visit Morocco without a visa for up to 90 days for tourism (UK FCDO, read 20.11.2026). For other nationalities, check with the Moroccan embassy or consulate whether your passport needs a visa.

  2. Find out if you can use the online e-visa

    Morocco has run an online e-visa for eligible nationalities that need a visa. Whether you are eligible, and the conditions that apply, must be confirmed with the official portal or your Moroccan consulate. We could not open the official portal on 20.11.2026, so we do not quote its rules here.

  3. Check your passport validity

    Sources differ on how much validity you need. For UK travellers the passport must expire at least 3 months after arrival (UK FCDO). Most travellers should plan for 3 to 6 months: check your embassy before you apply.

  4. Gather your documents and details

    Use the list below, then ask the official portal or consulate for the exact documents and photo specification they require. Do this before you start the form.

  5. Fill in the application on the official portal only

    Enter your details exactly as they appear in your passport. Check every field against the passport before you submit. Look for the official government portal and avoid look-alike sites that add charges.

  6. Keep your confirmation and travel documents together

    Save the confirmation, and carry your passport and approvals with you. On arrival, make sure your passport is stamped: travellers without a stamp have had trouble leaving.

What to prepare

Have ready

  • Your passport, valid long enough for your embassy's rule
  • Your travel dates and where you will stay
  • A digital photo, as the official portal specifies
  • A way to pay the official fee, shown on the portal

Good to know on arrival

  • Foreign currency of 100,000 MAD or more must be declared on entry or exit
  • A polio vaccination certificate is required if arriving from a polio-affected country
  • Never post your passport to or through Morocco: it is illegal and it can be confiscated
  • Stays beyond 90 days need an extension request at a Moroccan police station
