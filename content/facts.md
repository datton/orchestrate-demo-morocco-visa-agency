# Morocco: facts for the site

Checked 20.11.2026 by the Ghana Travel & Foreign Affairs Expert. General information, not legal or immigration advice.
**Rule for builders:** use only the "SAFE TO STATE" lines as facts. Everything under "CHECK WITH THE EMBASSY" must be worded as "check with the embassy / official portal", never as a promise. Never quote a price for the e-visa as the official fee. The agency's own service fee is the agency's own and is not covered here.

| Fact | Source |
|---|---|

## CHECK WITH THE EMBASSY (secondary or conflicting sources only, not confirmed)

- **Which nationalities need a visa.** Guides say about 78 nationalities are visa-free (reportedly incl. Canada, EU states, US, UK). No official list could be opened (Moroccan MFA and portal returned errors on 20.11.2026). Wording: "Check whether your passport needs a visa with the Moroccan embassy or consulate."
- **E-visa.** Morocco runs an online e-visa for eligible non-exempt nationalities (official portal reported as acces-maroc.ma; unreachable on 20.11.2026). Reported but UNCONFIRMED: single entry, stay up to 30 days, valid about 180 days from issue, ordinary passports only, standard fee reported as 770 MAD and express 1,100 MAD, processing about 72 working hours standard / 24 hours express. Sources conflict and are private sites. Do not state these as facts.
- **Passport validity.** Sources conflict: 3 months after arrival (UK FCDO, confirmed for UK), 6 months (some guides), more than 90 days on the day of e-visa application (e-visa guide). Wording: "Most travellers need at least 3 to 6 months of validity: check your embassy." Two blank pages is a common recommendation, UNCONFIRMED.
- **Canada.** Canadian government advice (travel.gc.ca) could not be reached on 20.11.2026: no Canada-specific claim is verified.
- **Health form.** One guide says the passenger health form is no longer required. UNCONFIRMED.
- Embassy visa fees, processing times and document lists: not retrieved. Do not state any.

## Wording guidance for a visa-help agency
- Say the agency helps with forms, checklists and guidance and is **not** the Moroccan government or an embassy; the visa decision is Morocco's.
- Always link or point to the official portal and the Moroccan consulate for final rules, and show "Rules checked 20.11.2026".
- No guarantees of approval or speed.
